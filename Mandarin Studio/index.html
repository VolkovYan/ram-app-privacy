Privacy Policy

Android app app.ramsolutionsmm.mandarinstudio

Learning data on your device

Mandarin Studio stores your practice dates, stroke results, lesson completion, and app preferences in private app storage. It uses those records to show your progress and remember your settings. The app compares your drawn strokes with a guide on your device and stores the result. It does not save the drawing itself.

Data collection and sharing

RAM Solutions does not receive your learning records or preferences from the app. Mandarin Studio does not require an account and has no advertising, analytics, or cloud-sync service. It does not access the microphone, camera, contacts, or location.

Pronunciation playback

Pinyin recordings bundled with the app play locally. When you request spoken text, the app sends the lesson text and speech rate to your device’s selected text-to-speech engine. Depending on the engine and voice you select, that provider may process the lesson text online under its own privacy policy. A locally installed offline Mandarin voice can play supported lessons without an internet connection.

Retention, backups, and deletion

Learning records and preferences remain in app storage until you clear the app’s storage or uninstall it. On Android, open Settings → Apps → Mandarin Studio → Storage → Clear storage to reset local data. Android may back up or restore eligible app data according to your device’s settings. Manage any system backups separately through your device or backup provider.

Privacy contact

For privacy questions, contact RAM Solutions using the email listed under App support on the Mandarin Studio Google Play listing.
